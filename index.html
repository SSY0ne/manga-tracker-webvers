Manga Tracker

Never wonder if your manga is on break.

A tracker that tells you when your manga is on break. It compares seven sources (MangaDex, MangaUpdates, MangaBaka, Webtoon, Kitsu, AniList and Shikimori), learns each series' own release rhythm, and a daily bot posts the news. Open source, with tests.

      What do the labels mean?
      New
      A chapter came out in the last 7 days.
      On break
      Much longer than usual without a chapter, measured against this series' own rhythm, or listed as on hiatus.
      Releasing
      Still being published, with nothing unusual: not new this week and not on break.
      ≠
      The sources that fit disagree by 2 chapters or more (for example 719 to 725). For a finished series Wikipedia's chapter list counts as one more opinion, shown as a reference and never used as the answer. Open the series to see each source's number and which one is shown. A difference of 1 is normal and only appears inside.
      Dormant
      No new chapter for 5 years or more. Still listed as running, but the author has probably stopped and may never return. Not counted as "on break".
      Finished
      The series has ended.
      Limited data
      No source has data that makes sense for it, so nothing is claimed.
      +12
      Chapters you still have to read (from your own reading progress).
